Coming from Harmony

Most of what you use every day in Harmony has a counterpart in Phoenix. This table says where to find it.

In HarmonyIn Phoenix
Xsheet viewWindow > X-Sheet
PegsInsert > Peg
Master controllersWindow > Controllers
Curve and envelope deformersModify > Deformer
Function viewWindow > Function Curves
Light tableView > Light Table (Shift+L)
Multiplane camera, Z depthInsert > Camera and Window > Layer Depth
Line art and color artInsert > Color Art Layer
Palettes and palette stylesWindow > Palettes
Tone, Highlight and Glow effectsInsert > Effect Layer
Render passesExport Movie with passes or multi-layer EXR
Lip-sync detectionModify > Lip Sync
Bones and kinematic chainsBone tool (M), for armatures of symbols
Brush presets and textured brushesBrush Properties and Presets on the Tool tab of the Properties panel
Repaint BrushA brush in Repaint mode
Storyboard Pro animaticFile > Import > Import Animatic (EDL or Final Cut XML)
Batch renderingWindow > Render Queue, or Phoenix render

Phoenix saves Phoenix documents (.phx), FLA files and XFL folders rather than Harmony scenes. What only Phoenix draws (deformers, effect layers, palettes) is kept in the file for Phoenix, and the rest opens anywhere FLA files do.
