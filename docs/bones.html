Bones and inverse kinematics

An armature is a chain of bones inside a shape or between symbols. Drag the end of the chain and the joints bend to follow, which is called inverse kinematics (IK). Phoenix plays and poses armatures made in Animate.

Armature layers

Armatures live on their own layers, and their timeline spans are drawn in a distinct color with a marker at each pose. A pose is a keyframe for the whole armature: Phoenix fills in the frames between poses, easing them the way the file says.

Posing

Select the shape to show its bones, drawn in the layer's color as slim shapes with round joints. Then, with the Selection tool:

  1. Move the playhead to the frame you want to pose.
  2. Drag a bone. Its tip follows the cursor and the bones above it in the chain bend to reach.

The frame becomes a pose if it wasn't one already. Each pose change is a single step to undo.

Symbol armatures and Asset Warp

  • Armatures between symbols show their bones and carry the instances with them.
  • Asset Warp puppets, where artwork bends around pins, are drawn deformed by their pins.

All armature and warp data is kept when you save, so files go back to Animate intact.
