Bones and inverse kinematics

An armature is a chain of bones inside a shape or between symbols. Drag the end of the chain and the joints bend to follow, which is called inverse kinematics (IK). Build armatures of symbols with the Bone tool, and pose and play them along with the armatures in FLA files.

Building an armature

Use the Bone tool (M) to join symbol instances with bones:

  1. Drag from a point on one instance to a point on another. The bone runs from where you press to where you let go.
  2. Phoenix puts both instances on a new armature layer, Armature_1, above the first one's layer. Each instance turns about its joint, which becomes its transformation point.
  3. To grow the armature, drag from any instance already in it to another instance. The new bone starts at that instance's joint, so one instance can branch into several bones. Bones can be added while the armature has a single pose, and an instance can be in only one armature.

Each bone added is a single step to undo.

Joint limits

Click a bone with the Bone tool, without dragging, to set how far the joint at its head may turn. In the Joint Rotation dialog, turn on Constrain and give the Min and Max angles, measured from the bone above. When you drag the armature into a pose, a limited joint stops at its limit and the other joints turn further to make up the difference.

Armature layers

Armatures live on their own layers, and their timeline spans are drawn in a distinct color with a marker at each pose. A pose is a keyframe for the whole armature: Phoenix fills in the frames between poses, easing them the way the file says.

Posing

Bones are drawn in the layer's color as slim shapes with round joints; for a shape armature, select the shape to show them. Then, with the Selection tool:

  1. Move the playhead to the frame you want to pose.
  2. Drag a bone. Its tip follows the cursor and the bones above it in the chain bend to reach.

The frame becomes a pose if it wasn't one already. Each pose change is a single step to undo.

Symbol armatures and Asset Warp

  • Armatures between symbols show their bones and carry the instances with them. One with a single pose can be posed from any frame of its span.
  • Asset Warp puppets, where artwork bends around pins, are drawn deformed by their pins, vector and bitmap art alike.

Asset Warp tool

Use the Asset Warp tool (Shift+W) to bend a puppet by its pins:

  • Click the puppet to show its pins. A click on the art where there is no pin adds one there.
  • Drag a pin. The art bends around the other pins, which hold their places.
  • Alt-click a pin, or click it and press Delete, to take it away.

Each pin change is a single step to undo. Pins are kept on each keyframe's instance, so a classic tween between two keyframes bends the art from one set of pins to the other. To bend art that is not a puppet, use a deformer.

All armature and warp data is kept when you save, so saved FLA files keep them intact.
